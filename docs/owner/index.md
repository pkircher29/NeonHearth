# NeonHearth owner guide

NeonHearth watches the home network you administer — locally, on one computer you choose — and this guide explains how to live with it. Start with [Getting started](getting-started.md) for what NeonHearth is, the safety boundary, and what the first 48 hours mean. [Devices and identity](devices-and-identity.md) explains how NeonHearth decides what a device is and how sure it is. [Guard and policy](guard-and-policy.md) covers approval deadlines, quarantine, and what enforcement really does (and does not do). [Home Twin](home-twin.md) covers drawing your home and the live 3D view. [Cameras and security](cameras-and-security.md) covers camera detection, live viewing, and security findings. [Privacy and data](privacy-and-data.md) states exactly what is stored, where, and what ever leaves the machine. [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md) walks through the failure modes the current software can actually produce. Every page ends with a "Not yet available" list so you always know what this build does not do yet.
